03The reference corpus

Every row names its sources and carries a status. None is VERIFIED: that needs a named human reviewer, and the database enforces it. Dispositions of every input, duplicates removed and exclusions are in docs/INTEGRATION_LEDGER.md.

Benefit Vault · migration 0014 · /api/v1/vault

Federal, state, nonprofit and retail benefits for service members, Guard and Reserve, veterans and families, from research ledgers read page by page. A deterministic matcher answers in the v6 outcome words and never decides; in production nothing shows as a match until a named reviewer verifies it (G1). Generated from data/reference/benefit_vault_registry.json; defects VD01–VD22 are in docs/BENEFIT_VAULT_ARCHITECTURE.md and ADR 0005.

04Governance

Four non-negotiables

Claims legal lane (38 CFR 14.629, 14.636; 38 U.S.C. 5901). VetBuddy may inform, calculate, organize and refer. It may never prepare, sign, submit or argue a claim, ITF or review, and nothing claims-related may carry a fee. The GUARD VA Benefits Act (H.R. 1732) is pending; build as if it were enacted.

07Launch scope

Launch 0 · vetbuddy.com, static

A guest-only Alabama finder evaluated in the browser. It shows "verification required" until G1 closes, crisis help on every page, and real privacy, terms and accessibility pages. The standalone guest app already meets these privacy gates.

Launch 1 · app.vetbuddy.com

  • Authentication, profile and progressive intake
  • Master Case, tasks, deadlines, documents
  • Engine-backed Alabama discovery
  • ResourceHub (fed by the reference corpus)
  • Read-only claims organization
  • Constrained AI with a static router
  • Audit, account deletion, consent controls

Never, at any phase: storing an SSN; presenting AI output as an official determination; claiming a certification not held; presenting uncertain eligibility as guaranteed; presenting stale information as current.

11Next steps, in order

  1. Close G1. Name the verifier and verify the eight Alabama rules. Then the highest-risk reference rows: the 13 MST residential programs (2009 source), the G.I. Dependents' cap, 2026 VA and BAS rates, and the statutory timeline windows.
  2. Recover the v2.0 source, or re-transcribe the rating schedule from current eCFR.
  3. Decide G17 (identity provider and token model). It unblocks every remaining account release blocker in v6 §2–3: verification, recovery, MFA, step-up before deletion and the public deletion request, and the vault's account routes in production.
  4. Vault follow-through. Decide VD06 and VD21; then wire pull providers over the slice 03 stores, move vault store calls off the event loop (VD22), and start G1 review with the Alabama records and the 47 SOURCE_CHECKED rows.
  5. Slice 05. Outbox dispatcher with idempotent consumers (deletion already purges queued events), an axe and assistive-technology accessibility pass, and the v6 legal applicability register seeded with UNKNOWN rows for all 56 jurisdictions.
  6. Before any career module: the ADA metamorphic test and the fact-level allowlist (CB-01/02). The work-style assessment needs an instrument or licence decision first.
  7. Later, education-only content: insurance (VGLI/SGLI, no product rankings) and a regulatory change feed for VA law history, never a static page.
